Questions

How are sends counted?

Each recipient in a send counts as one email against your monthly limit — dry-runs included, so the meter is honest.

Can I change plans anytime?

Yes. Upgrades and downgrades take effect immediately from your Billing page.

Do you charge a card?

This deployment runs in demo mode — plan changes apply instantly with no card. Stripe Checkout drops into the same flow in production.

Is my data isolated?

Every account has its own campaigns, recipients, and templates, scoped by user id at the database layer.
