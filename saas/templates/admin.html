How to get a Gmail App Password (2 minutes)

  1. Go to myaccount.google.com/security.
  2. Turn on 2-Step Verification if it isn't already (App Passwords require it).
  3. Open App passwords, type a name like MailPilot, and click Create.
  4. Google shows a 16-character password — copy it.
  5. Paste it into the Gmail App Password box above with the matching Gmail address, click Save, then Send test email.

Gmail sends roughly 500 emails/day on a free account (about 2,000/day on Google Workspace). Plan quotas keep customers within that.
