Sl. No. 03 — About

Small enough to know your shop by name.

Marklo is a Kolkata-based agency built around one idea: local businesses don't need a 200-person agency reciting global case studies — they need someone who understands Ballygunge foot traffic, Durga Puja seasonality, and what makes a Kolkata customer trust a business online.

Why we started

Too many small businesses in Kolkata were being sold generic "digital marketing packages" that never mentioned their actual product, customers, or neighbourhood. Marklo exists to run marketing the way a good accountant runs your books — specific to your business, and accountable line by line.

Who we work with

Jewellery showrooms, restaurants and food brands, and coaching institutes make up most of our client base — though our service list covers any local business that needs to be found online and convert enquiries into customers.
