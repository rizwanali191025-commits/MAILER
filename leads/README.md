# Marklo — Kolkata Cold-Call Lead List

15 real, named businesses across Marklo's three target verticals (jewellery,
restaurants, coaching institutes), each with a phone number and address
sourced from the business's own website, Facebook page, or a public
directory listing. **No entry is invented** — every name/phone/address came
from a live source (see the `Source` column in the CSV).

## Files
- `marklo-kolkata-cold-call-list.csv` — the list, with tracking columns
  (`Called (Y/N)`, `Call Date`, `Outcome`) to fill in as you work through it.

## Coverage & honest limitations

- **15 leads** is a starter list, not a full database. Business directories
  like JustDial actively block automated access (I hit 403s trying), so this
  was built from search-engine snippets and businesses' own public sites —
  a slower, manual-style process, not bulk scraping.
- A few entries are branches of the same business (Saptapadi, India
  Restaurant) — call once per business, not once per branch, unless you want
  to reach that specific location's owner/manager.
- I deliberately **left out big national/regional chains** (Tanishq, Senco
  Gold, Kalyan Jewellers, Malabar Gold, Aakash Institute, etc.) — they
  already run their own large-scale marketing and are poor-fit prospects for
  a small local agency. Adyama Gold is a borderline case (regional, ~17
  branches) but kept in as still plausibly reachable.
- Phone numbers are current as of this research (Jul 2026) but not
  independently called to verify — always confirm on first contact.

## Getting more volume

To scale past 15, options that don't involve bypassing directory
anti-bot protection:
1. **Manual browsing** — log into JustDial/Sulekha yourself and copy
   listings; they show full details to logged-in human users.
2. **JustDial/IndiaMART lead-gen products** — both sell verified local
   business contact databases directly; this is the sanctioned way to get
   bulk data from them.
3. **Local trade associations** — Kolkata jewellers', restaurant, and
   coaching-institute associations often publish member directories.
4. Ask me to keep researching in smaller batches via search — slower, but
   it's genuine public data each time.

## Suggested opening line (matches Marklo's brand voice)

Marklo's own site uses a "ledger/khata" (accounting book) metaphor — "every
rupee you spend on marketing should show up in the khata." A cold-call
opener consistent with that:

> "Hi, I'm calling from Marklo — we help Kolkata [jewellery/restaurant/
> coaching] businesses make sure their marketing spend actually shows up as
> new customers, not just impressions. Do you currently track where your
> enquiries are coming from?"
